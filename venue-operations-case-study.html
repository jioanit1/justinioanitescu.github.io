The Question

What does a busy event actually tell us about the fan experience?

Attendance alone was not enough. The analysis compared traffic, engagement, spend, and experience signals across event days to understand where the venue performed efficiently — and where operational changes could matter more than simply attracting more people.

Operational Read

Lower-traffic dates still created meaningful per-fan value, while some of the strongest commercial days did not produce the highest engagement. The useful output was not just reporting — it was guidance for staffing, activation placement, fan-flow adjustments, and post-event review.
