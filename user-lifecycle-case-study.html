User Lifecycle & Commercial Analytics

A de-identified BI framework for a digital soccer platform, designed to show where users enter, where they drop, what behavior drives repeat use, and which metrics management should trust.

SettingDigital soccer platform
FocusUser lifecycle + commercial action

Operating View

Growth is useful only if users move through the product.

The core model followed the user journey from acquisition through activation, verification, activity, and retention. Every major drop-off needed to be diagnosable by source, campaign, market, and user segment.

What Changed

The focus moved from total users to what happens after acquisition: verification, repeat behavior, social play, and retention. That shifted the management conversation from top-of-funnel volume to product quality and lifecycle health.

Trust the definitions first.

Lifecycle states and deduplication rules need to be stable before trend analysis is meaningful.

Diagnose by segment.

Every major drop should be traceable by source, market, campaign, or platform.

Connect insight to an owner.

Product, Marketing, Sales, and Technology should see which action belongs to them.
