Case Study 03 · Global Football / Strategic Research

Player Development & Strategic Benchmarking

A de-identified research project comparing player-development systems, academy models, market structures, and competitive approaches for a global football organization evaluating North American opportunities.

SettingGlobal football organization
FocusDevelopment + market intelligence
MethodBenchmarking · KPI design · strategic research
NoteAcademy names and specific recommendations are generalized.
The Assignment

Compare development models without reducing them to league tables.

The work examined how leading academies and sports organizations structure talent pipelines, player-development environments, operating models, and market reach. The goal was not to declare a single “best” academy, but to identify transferable practices and where they fit.

Market View

Separate development readiness from commercial opportunity.

A market can be commercially attractive without having the same talent-development infrastructure — and vice versa. Treating those as separate axes made prioritization more useful.
